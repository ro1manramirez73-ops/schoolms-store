Questions
Frequently asked

Does it run without the internet?

Yes. After the initial install, the app runs completely offline. Staff access it through your local network — no cloud needed.

Can multiple computers use it at the same time?

Yes. Install it on one computer (your "server") and every other device on the same Wi-Fi can open it in any browser — phones, tablets, laptops.

How many students can I add?

Unlimited students in all plans. The staff account limits only apply to login accounts, not student records.

What operating system is required?

Windows 10 or Windows 11. The server computer runs the app; every other device just needs a browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

Is there a subscription or renewal fee?

No. You pay once and own that version forever. Future major updates may be offered at a discounted upgrade price.

What's included in the payroll module?

Full payroll management — salaries, NIB contributions, deductions (bank loans, credit union, etc.), loan tracking, payslip PDFs, and monthly payroll reports. Staff added in the school system automatically appear in payroll.

Do you offer support?

Yes. Email support is included with all plans. Standard and Professional plans have priority response. Professional also includes a remote setup call.

Can I get a refund?

If the software cannot be installed or run on your computer within 14 days, contact us for a full refund. We'll first try to resolve any issues remotely.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. v2.0 includes full English and Spanish support. Any user can switch the language from the sidebar with a single click — the entire interface updates instantly with no restart required.

What's new in v2.0?

v2.0 adds: full English/Spanish bilingual interface, a built-in role-based user manual (PDF) accessible from every user's dashboard, automatic 10-digit unique ID generation for students and staff, and a unified ID system across students, staff, and payroll.
