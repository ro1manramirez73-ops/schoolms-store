Optional — Remote Access
Access your school from anywhere

By default SchoolMS runs on your local Wi-Fi. If you want staff, parents, or students to connect from outside campus, you can set up your own Cloudflare Tunnel — giving your system a real HTTPS URL accessible from anywhere. You create and manage your own Cloudflare account; we are not involved in that connection.

  • ✅ Staff access from home or phone

How to go online with your own Cloudflare Tunnel
You create and own your Cloudflare account · Takes about 10 minutes · No technical skills needed
1
Create your own Cloudflare account
Go to cloudflare.com, create your own account (free), and add your domain. This account is yours — we have no access to it.
2
Run the setup script
Double-click setup_cloudflare.bat included in your install. It downloads cloudflared, logs in, and configures everything automatically.
3
Enter your domain
The script asks for your hostname, e.g. school.yourdomain.com, then creates the DNS record automatically.
✓
Done — you're live
Your school system is now at https://school.yourdomain.com — accessible from any device, anywhere. The tunnel restarts automatically on reboot.
💡 Cloudflare accounts are free. You only need a domain (~$10/year from Namecheap or Google Domains). Your tunnel, your account — we don't manage it for you.

System requirements

SchoolMS runs on the computer that acts as your school's server. Client devices (phones, tablets, laptops) only need a browser.

🖥️
Operating System
Windows 10 or 11
64-bit only. The server computer must run Windows. Client devices can be anything with a browser.
⚙️
Processor
Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3
Or any 64-bit dual-core at 1.8 GHz or faster. Any computer made after 2016 will comfortably run the app.
🧠
Memory (RAM)
4 GB minimum  ·  8 GB recommended
4 GB is enough for the school system. 8 GB is recommended if you also run the payroll module at the same time.

10 GB free minimum
~700 MB for the app and Python runtime. The rest grows with your data — student photos, invoices, receipts, and payroll records over time.
📶

📱

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. Works on Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows — no app install needed on client devices.

Questions
Frequently asked

Does it run without the internet?

Yes. After install, the app runs completely offline. Staff access it through your local network — no cloud required.

Can multiple computers use it at once?

Yes. Install on one computer (your "server") and every device on the same Wi-Fi opens it in any browser.

How many students can I add?

Unlimited students in all plans. Staff account limits only apply to login accounts, not student records.

What OS is required?

Windows 10 or 11 on the server. Other devices just need a browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.

Is there a subscription or renewal fee?

No. Pay once and own that version forever. Future major updates may be available at a discounted upgrade price.

What's in the payroll module?

Salaries, NIB contributions, deductions, loan tracking, payslip PDFs, and monthly payroll reports. Staff sync automatically from the school roster.

Do you offer support?

Yes. Email support in all plans. Standard and Professional have priority response. Professional includes a remote setup call.

Can I get a refund?

If the software can't run on your computer within 14 days, contact us for a full refund. We'll try to resolve it remotely first.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. v2.0 includes full English and Spanish support. Switch language from the sidebar with one click — no restart needed.

Can staff access it from home or outside school?

Yes — using your own Cloudflare Tunnel. You create a free Cloudflare account at cloudflare.com, add your domain, then run the setup_cloudflare.bat script included with SchoolMS. It sets everything up automatically and gives your system a real HTTPS URL like school.yourdomain.com. We are not connected to your tunnel — you own and manage your Cloudflare account entirely. See the Go Online section for the full guide.

What's new in v2.0?

Full bilingual interface, built-in role-based user manuals (PDF), automatic 10-digit ID generation for students and staff, database optimizations (up to 10× faster), and a /health endpoint for uptime monitoring.
