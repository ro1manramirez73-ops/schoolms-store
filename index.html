Questions

Frequently asked

Does it run without the internet? +
Yes. After the initial install, the app runs completely offline on your local network. No cloud needed, no data sent anywhere.
Can multiple computers use it at the same time? +
Yes. Install it on one computer (your “server”) and every other device on the same Wi-Fi can open it in any browser — phones, tablets, laptops, all at once.
How many students can I add? +
Unlimited students in all plans. The staff account limits only apply to login accounts, not to student records.
What operating system is required? +
Windows 10 or Windows 11 on the server computer. Every other device — including Macs, phones, tablets — just needs a browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
Is there a subscription or renewal fee? +
No. You pay once and own that version forever. Future major updates may be offered at a discounted upgrade price, but the software you buy never expires.
What’s included in the payroll module? +
Full payroll management — salaries, NIB contributions, deductions (bank loans, credit union, etc.), loan tracking, payslip PDFs, and monthly payroll reports. Staff added to the school system automatically appear in payroll.
Do you offer support? +
Yes. Email support is included with all plans. Standard and Professional plans have priority response. Professional also includes a remote setup call so we can walk you through the configuration live.
Can I get a refund? +
If the software cannot be installed or run on your computer within 14 days of purchase, contact us for a full refund. We’ll first try to resolve any issues remotely.
Is it available in Spanish? +
Yes. v2.0 includes full English and Spanish support. Any user can switch the language from the sidebar with a single click — the entire interface updates instantly with no restart required.
What’s new in v2.0? +
v2.0 adds: full English/Spanish bilingual interface, a built-in role-based user manual (PDF) from every dashboard, automatic 10-digit unique ID generation for students and staff, database query optimizations (up to 10× faster on large datasets), 14 query indexes, per-request connection pooling, rotating server logs, a /health endpoint for uptime monitoring, automatic slow-request detection, and background email delivery so the UI never freezes.
